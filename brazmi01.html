Scouting Report
His ability to create shots for himself off the dribble has improved... Has many effective moves in the low post including a go-to jump hook. Has even developed a sky hook... His free throw shooting must improve... Has great ball handling and can push the ball up the floor better than most players his size... Positions himself well for rebounds and excels on the offensive glass... Good help defender... He is not too aggressive but not afraid either... Remarkably strong build, which helps him absorb contact and finish near the rim

His passing ability has shown some improvement but he lacks great vision to find teammates off the dribble
